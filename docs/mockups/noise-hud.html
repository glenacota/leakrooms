W A S D or arrows move · SHIFT sprint · hold E for the wrench (the plumber stands still, as when repairing). With no key for 2.5 s a demo plays: walk, sprint, stop, wade through water, wrench. In SLIDER mode the slider sets the noise and the plumber just walks.

What is exact. Noise levels (walk 0.20, wade 0.30, LOUD 0.50, sprint 0.70, wrench 0.85), the ring's full scale (0.85), the 1.55 per second decay, the walk and sprint speeds (3.3 and 6.0 tiles/s, wading at 80%), the 0.15 s fade in and 0.4 s fade out above 0.02, the 80% HUD opacity, the camera lag, and the radar ring and today's noise ring (ported from diegetic.js, same offsets and sizes). One tile is 48 px here, which is the game at 1280x720.

What is simplified. The plumber sprite, the walls and carpet, no lighting, and water that is on or off per tile rather than a gradient.

Opacity. With "opacity follows noise" on, the level indicator's opacity is noise / 0.85, so a dry walk (0.20) is about 24% of the normal 80%. Reference marks (tracks, LOUD notches, guide lines, housings) sit at half of the plain 80%, so you can still find them.
