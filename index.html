ADVICES

  • In the Backrooms, the quieter you are, the safer you stay.
  • Follow the wet carpet to identify the source of the leak.
  • Pipe reparation requires 5 seconds of loud wrench work. Remain nearby until completion.
  • Your gear makes running loud and exhausting. Walk to regain stamina.
  • Activate the radar to reveal the floor plan. Charges burn out in 10 seconds, so keep an eye out for spare batteries.
  • Loud actions can wake or attract hostile lifeforms. Running, radar use, and repairs are loud. Walking is quieter and recommended.
  • If something chases you, flee the area fast. Keep quiet once you're out of sight to lose your pursuer.
