RULES

  • FOLLOW THE WATER. Wetter floor means a leak is closer.
  • SEAL A LEAK. Stand on the spot below it and hold still for 5 seconds. Moving drains the bar. Sealing is loud.
  • USE THE RADAR. It shows the floor map, not the leaks. It drains the battery. Pick up spare batteries.
  • LEAVE BY THE DOOR. It opens only when every leak is sealed.
  • BEAT THE CLOCK. You have 3 minutes per floor. At zero you lose a life.
